    How detection decides

    Every 10 seconds

    While a print runs, one camera frame goes to Obico's detector. It draws a box around anything that looks like spaghetti; the bars add up how sure it is.

    The score

    The line smooths the bars over the last minutes and subtracts what is usual for your camera, so a single odd frame or a reflection that is always there doesn't count. Around 0 means nothing unusual.

    The two levels

    Above the warning level you're notified. Above the pause level, Pause mode pauses the print; Observe mode only tells you it would have. Both levels adapt a little to the print.

    Your answer

    Looks fine dismisses the alert and keeps watching. Mute stops alerts until the print ends. The first minutes of a print never alert.
